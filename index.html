👮‍♂️ How to become a Moderator?

We are glad you want to join the Sword Dungeon Piece team. You will start as an Officer.

Requirements:

  • Age: 13+ (or turning 13 within 3 weeks).
  • Account age: At least 1 year.
  • Server stay: At least 1 week.
  • Knowledge of rules is mandatory.
  • Stay calm and polite under pressure.

📌 Rules for Moderators:

  • 1.1 Be respectful to players.
  • 1.2 No swearing in official chats.
  • 1.3 Stay out of conflicts.
  • 1.4 No bribes (instant ban).
  • 1.5 Do not abuse powers.
  • 1.6 Use format: /timeout [user] [time] [reason].
  • 1.7 You have 3 lives (warnings).
